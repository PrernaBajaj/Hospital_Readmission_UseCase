# Recommendation for the Care-Management Lead

To help you and your team make the most of your outreach capacity each week, we have put together a priority list that identifies 5% of the discharged patients most likely to return to the hospital.

The percentage next to each admission_ID is a calibrated probability, meaning a score of 0.85 implies 85 of 100 similar patients were readmitted without proactive support. 

Along with the risk score, the list provides key clinical factors contributing to each patient's risk classification, including the number of recorded chronic conditions, age, previous hospital admissions, discharge medication count, and length of hospital stay, among other relevant indicators.

We recommend initiating a 2-4 week pilot to evaluate the effectiveness of the proposed system. While the model identifies patients at higher risk of readmission, it does not establish whether follow-up calls can prevent it. As a comparison, we can also create a shortlist of the top 5% of patients identified through clinical judgement. Comparing readmission rates across the two groups will help assess the effectiveness of the model-driven approach and guide decisions on scaling the programme.

This tool only sees what is documented in the medical records at the time of discharge, please use your clinical knowledge and intuition as the final filter to decide who gets the call, and let us know if the tool flags anyone who doesn't actually need intervention so that we can continuously improve the model.
